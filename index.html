Why parents choose Elyss

The five things parents ask us about — answered honestly.

Most parents arrive with the same worries: is the CBE real here, is my child safe, who is actually teaching, what will this cost, and will my child be noticed. Here is where we stand on each.

CBE that you can actually see

We follow Kenya’s CBE curriculum from Playgroup to Grade 6, with play-based learning balanced by structured, age-appropriate work. Ask us to walk you through a normal day for your child’s class — we’ll show you the routine, not a syllabus document.

Safety that is planned, not promised

Our school transport runs on NTSA-approved vehicles with certified drivers, on routes planned around your estate. Pick-up and drop-off times are confirmed with you and kept updated — so you are never left wondering where your child is.

Teachers your child’s name is safe with

Our classes are small enough that every learner is known — their pace, their strengths, where they get stuck. When you visit, you meet the people who will teach your child, and you can ask them your specific question directly.

Fees explained clearly, before you commit

Fees vary by class and by whether your child uses transport. Tell us your child’s age or current class and we’ll send a clear, easy-to-understand breakdown — no hidden line items discovered in week three.

A Christian foundation, lived daily

Elyss is a Christian learning centre. Character, honesty and kindness are part of the school day rather than an assembly topic — and they show up in how our learners treat each other.

A real person answers

Call or WhatsApp 0711 650 405 and you reach our team, not a queue. Ask about fees, transport, a visit date, or simply what your child’s week would look like.

Learning at Elyss

Every stage built so the next one feels natural, not rushed.

Our CBE programme is designed to grow with your child, step by step — from a first day in Playgroup to the move into Junior School.

Ages 3–5

Playgroup & Kindergarten

A child’s first real relationship with learning. Play-based days, language and number foundations, and an environment they are excited to talk about at home.

  • Settling-in support for first-time schoolers
  • Sensory, play and outdoor learning corners
  • Available at both campuses

Grades 1–3

Lower Primary

Reading, writing and number sense laid down properly, with the structured practice that makes later work possible — at a pace that suits the child in front of us.

  • Literacy and numeracy fundamentals
  • Hands-on CBE activities and projects
  • Regular, plain-language progress updates

Grades 4–6

Upper Primary

Independent thinking, real projects and preparation for KPSEA — learners who can do the work and explain their reasoning out loud.

  • Project-based, competency-led learning
  • Grade 6 assessment preparation
  • Deliberate bridge into Junior School

Grades 7–9 · Thogoto

Junior School (JSS)

The jump into JSS is one of the biggest transitions in a child’s schooling. Our team plans for it on purpose — building the bridge rather than hoping learners adjust.

  • New subjects introduced with structure and support
  • Abacus & mental maths built into the programme
  • Creative art with locally available materials

What makes an Elyss learner different

Abacus & mental maths

Not an after-school add-on — it is how our learners build number sense from the inside out. A child who moves the beads, then answers without them, has learned to see numbers rather than memorise them.

Creative art, made by hand

Our learners make things from locally available materials — and can explain exactly how. Practical, capable work you can watch happen, not just a finished product on a shelf.

Character you can notice

Christian values, respect and resilience are taught in the ordinary moments of the school day. Most parents tell us they see it at home before they see it on a report.

Admissions

Joining Elyss takes four straightforward steps.

We welcome inquiries throughout the school year. Start wherever you are — most parents begin with a visit.

  1. 1

    Inquire & visit

    Tell us your child’s age or current class and the term you’re hoping to join. We’ll arrange a day that works for you to see the classrooms and meet the team.

  2. 2

    Get your fee breakdown

    We send a clear breakdown for your child’s class, including transport if you need it, plus the requirements list — so you can decide with the full picture.

  3. 3

    Apply & meet the teachers

    Complete the admission form and bring your child in for a friendly, age-appropriate interaction so we can place them at the right level.

  4. 4

    Confirm your place

    You receive your offer and joining instructions, and we confirm your transport route and start date before day one.

Not ready to apply yet? That’s fine — call or WhatsApp 0711 650 405 and just ask your question.
