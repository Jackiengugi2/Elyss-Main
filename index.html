Before you decide

What parents ask us first.

“Is it really CBE?”

Yes. We follow Kenya’s CBE from Playgroup to Grade 6. Your child learns by doing — play, projects and hands-on work — not just books.

“How much are the fees?”

Fees depend on your child’s class and whether you need transport. Tell us the class and we’ll send you a clear breakdown — no surprises later.

“Do you offer transport?”

Yes. NTSA-approved vehicles and certified drivers, on a route planned around your estate. Tell us where you live and we’ll confirm your pick-up time.

“Can I see the school first?”

Please do. Pick a day that suits you, walk through the classrooms and meet the teachers. No booking fee, no pressure.

Still have a question? Ask us on WhatsApp or call 0711 650 405.
